Give XR games real AI vision.

ArcNode is a small hardware box that runs object detection next to your XR glasses, so games can see and react to the real world without draining your phone.

One email when pre-orders open. No spam.

Your glasses show it. ArcNode sees it.

Heavy vision work moves off the headset and phone, so the game stays smooth.

Capture

Camera frames from your XR setup stream to the ArcNode over your local network.

Detect

A dedicated AI accelerator runs detection in real time, close to where you play.

React

Results flow back to the game, which can place, hide or trigger things in your room.
